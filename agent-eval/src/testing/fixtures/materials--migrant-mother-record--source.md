# Library of Congress Migrant Mother collection guide

Source: https://guides.loc.gov/migrant-mother
Retrieved: 2026-10-11T13:39:42.197464+00:00

The photograph that has become known as "Migrant Mother" is one of a series of photographs that Dorothea Lange made of Florence Owens Thompson and her children in March of 1936 in Nipomo, California. Lange was concluding a month's trip photographing migratory farm labor around the state for what was then the Resettlement Administration.
